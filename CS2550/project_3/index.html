Nerd Culture Now is the place to go to see everything happening in the world of nerdy entertainment. From movies and TV shows to books and videogames, NCN is on top of the latest news for all of your favorite universes.

Spider-Man: Brand New Day

Brand New Day is the newest Spider-man and MCU movie coming to theaters July 31st. This marks Tom Holland's 4th solo movie as the web-slinger and his 7th in universe appearance.

Street Fighter

This beloved arcade fighting game hits the big screen once again. The hit and miss nature of this series' attempts at movies leaves many skeptical of this newest attempt hitting theaters October 16th. After seeing this trailer, I can't help but be optomistic about this latest venture into the wild and crazy world of Street Fighter.

The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping

The world-wide phenomenon returns to theaters for the 6th time November 20th. Suzanne Collins' dystopian world has been expanding recently and this latest adaptation looks to be the best one yet. Following fan-favorite Haymitch Abernathy, we'll get to see how he survived his games 25 years before the original series took place.

Halo: Campaign Evolved

Halo was one of my favorite childhood games. I didn't own an Xbox until Halo 3 though. The opportunity to experience the classic campaign in fully remastered detail is something I'm really looking forward to. Coming to Xbox, PS5 and PC July 28th.

Pokemon Winds and Pokemon Waves

Marking the 10th generation of Pokemon, Winds and Waves look to take the leap forward fans have been wanting for a while. With no set release date, we can expect more information to drop before this anticipated game hits Switch 2 consoles some time in 2027.

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4

While many believe Call of Duty has lost some of its original magic, the rebooted Modern Warfare series have been the highlights in recent years. Modern Warfare 4 looks to continue that trend on Xbox, PS5, Switch 2 and PC on October 23rd.

Lanterns

As a part of the new DC Universe, Lanterns looks to bring a new level of action and love to the popular Green Lantern characters Hal Jordan and John Stewart. This highly anticipated show premieres on HBO Max August 16th.

Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone

The world-wide phenomenon returns in a new format. With an anticipated 10 seasons, this HBO Harry Potter remake looks to capture the magic the original books and movies have found over the past 25 years. The new series will hit our screens on Christmas Day this year.

Avatar: The Last Airbender

Following a movie that most are happy to forget, the first season of this live action remake of a beloved cartoon blew away the audience and their expectations. Season 2, coming to Netflix June 25th, will introduce fan-favorite character Toph Beifong in her first live action appearance. Can the second season continue the trend of success?
